E-commerce-Funnel-Investigation
                                  This project predicts which customers are likely to churn (leave the service) using machine learning, and presents the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard. The goal is to help a business identify at-risk customers early and understand the key factors driving churn, so retention teams can act before it's too late.
Problem:
        "We're losing customers and don't know who's at risk until it's too late. Build a model that predicts which customers are likely to churn, so the retention team can reach out before they leave. Also explain WHY customers churn."
